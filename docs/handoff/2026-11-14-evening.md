Tavily searches and extracts sources, a Nebius model interprets the claim and judges the
evidence, and a verdict (True / False / Almost / unable to decide) comes out.

## Known gaps left on purpose
- A run that hits the deadline during the final check with a verdict pending still fails
  outright (two tests pin this). Left alone to keep the change small.
- If the model twice proposes a bad "Almost" correction, a sound True/False verdict is also
  withheld. Better: drop the bad correction and keep the checked verdict. Small follow-up.
- Withheld verdicts reuse the reason `insufficient_evidence`, which is slightly inaccurate.
  Adding a new reason would touch the public contract and the web client.
